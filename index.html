About Us

PlayfulOutings was created with one simple mission: to make every picnic and outdoor gathering more joyful and memorable. Based in Abuja, Nigeria, we believe great memories are made when families and friends play together in nature.

We curate fun, easy-to-play games for all ages and create simple getaway plans so you can focus on what matters most — spending quality time with loved ones.
